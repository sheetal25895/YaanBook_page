How pricing works

Why the same flight costs ₹4 lakh at one operator and ₹9 lakh at another

Charter operators bill for every hour the aircraft flies, including the empty flights to pick you up and to go back home. Ask a Mumbai operator for Delhi → Bengaluru and you pay for Mumbai → Delhi, Delhi → Bengaluru and Bengaluru → Mumbai.

YaanBook knows where each aircraft is based, so it calculates the full fare for every one of them and puts the true cheapest first. Usually that's an aircraft already at your departure city or close to it.

  • Positioning: the empty flight from the aircraft's base to your pickup.
  • Your flight: flight time at the aircraft's cruise speed, plus taxi and climb.
  • Return to base: charged on one-way trips unless the operator sells it as an empty leg.
  • Minimum billing: most operators bill at least 1 to 2 hours for each day they hold the aircraft for you.
  • Night halts, landing and handling for each stop away from base, then 18% GST.

Rates come from verified partners or, for other operators, from typical market rates for the model. Every booking is confirmed with a written quote before you pay anything.
